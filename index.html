Reference architecture

A layered observability architecture for data platforms

DataObs designs around a reference architecture that separates signal sources, collection, enrichment, analytics, and action.

01

Sources

AWS, databases, data pipelines, applications, AI agents, MCP servers, logs, metrics, traces, and events.

02

Collection

OpenTelemetry Collector, Elastic Agent, Fluent Bit, APIs, custom probes, and workflow-level instrumentation.

03

Processing

Enrichment, ownership mapping, business context, environment tags, dependency context, and correlation.

04

Storage and analytics

Elastic, OpenSearch, Grafana, Datadog, CloudWatch, SIEM, and other tools already operated by your teams.

05

Consumption

Dashboards, alerts, reports, runbooks, incident workflows, compliance evidence, and service review packs.

AI systems depend on trustworthy data, reliable pipelines, governed tool access, and explainable automation behaviour. DataObs helps teams observe the operational path from source data through AI agents to business outcomes.
