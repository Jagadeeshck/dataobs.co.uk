Outcome

The problem

Modern data platforms are harder to trust

Data teams are responsible for more pipelines, consumers, AI workflows, and business decisions than ever. Reliability signals often exist, but they are scattered across tools and hard to connect to business impact.

Dashboards show wrong or stale data.

Pipelines fail silently.

Schema changes break downstream consumers.

AI systems depend on data nobody is monitoring properly.

Security teams need visibility into sensitive data access.

Existing observability tools are not connected to data reliability.

About

Founder-led, practical, and vendor-neutral

DataObs is a founder-led specialist consultancy focused on practical data observability. It is built from hands-on platform engineering experience across cloud infrastructure, Elastic and OpenSearch, AWS data platforms, SAS environments, infrastructure automation, SIEM, and enterprise data operations.

The focus is on helping teams make better use of the tools they already operate, while improving the clarity, ownership, and actionability of their data reliability signals.
