The problem

Modern data platforms are harder to trust

Dashboards can look healthy while the data behind them is late, incomplete, duplicated, or no longer aligned to the business process.

AI systems and automated agents increase the risk because they depend on reliable inputs, governed tool access, and observable decisions.

DataObs connects data reliability signals with platform telemetry so teams can move from “something is wrong” to “this dataset, owner, job, and business SLA are affected.”
