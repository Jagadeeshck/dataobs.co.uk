Six-pillar product model

DataObs organises observability around the questions that matter to platform, data, and business teams.

1. Platform

What is deployed, where, and is it healthy? Hosts, containers, Kubernetes, serverless, and cloud data services.

2. Data Pipeline

Are jobs and streams running correctly and on time? Spark, dbt, Glue, Lambda, Kafka, and more.

3. Data

Can I trust my data? Freshness, volume, schema, nullness, uniqueness, distribution drift, and lineage.

4. FinOps & Cost

What is the platform costing, and why? Allocation, anomalies, and optimisation signals.

5. Business

Is the platform delivering value? SLAs, KPI impact, and data-product reliability.

6. AI & Agent

Can AI and autonomous agents be trusted, controlled, and audited?

Architecture principles

Elasticsearch-native

Data streams, versioned state indices, transforms, ES|QL, and Kibana for deep investigation. Elasticsearch is the system of record.

Open standards

OpenTelemetry for telemetry. OpenLineage for job and lineage events. Vendor-neutral ingestion by design.

Collection plane

Reuse Elastic Agent / Fleet / EDOT where possible. DataObs Scanner for data-specific scanning, profiling, and quality.
